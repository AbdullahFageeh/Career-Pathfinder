# Project rules
This project is an always-on opportunity engine, not only a standard job applier.

## Scope
- Optimize for well-paying, reliable opportunities rather than raw application volume.
- Search beyond the user’s original role family into adjacent higher-value roles, remote-friendly jobs, contract or freelance work, and other repeatable income paths that plausibly fit the user’s real background.
- Start with direct-fit operations, installation, production, site, and venue roles, then widen into adjacent and remote lanes when they reuse the same evidence base honestly.

## Safety and quality
- Treat community posts, social posts, and unofficial boards as discovery signals only; resolve leads to official employer or platform pages before acting on them when possible.
- Filter out scams, MLM-style offers, commission-only traps, and low-legitimacy income leads before they reach apply or outreach stages.
- Never invent experience, skills, ATS results, compensation facts, recruiter details, or contact data.

## Product behavior
- Keep data models, tracking, and workflows broad enough to handle both traditional job applications and non-job income opportunities.
- When a formal CV is not required, prefer outputs such as a short profile summary, outreach message, or capability snapshot instead of forcing a resume flow.
